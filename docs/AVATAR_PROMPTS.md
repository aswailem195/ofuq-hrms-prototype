# Photorealistic fictional employee portraits

Generated on 15 November 2026 with the built-in `image_gen` tool, with one independent generation per portrait. All people are entirely fictional. Photographs use a consistent pale blue-gray background and centered head-and-shoulders framing for circular UI avatars.

Final assets: `public/assets/avatars/avatar-1.webp` through `avatar-12.webp` (1254×1254). WebP files are encoded losslessly from the generated PNGs; pixel comparisons confirmed no visual-content changes. Original generated PNGs remain in the image tool’s generation library. The project stores only the final web assets.

Portrait slots are reused consistently across the fictional demo employees; this is a set of 12 portraits, not photographs of 48 different real people.

```text

Asset type: Individual professional HRMS employee avatar headshot, square 1:1 single image.

Composition and crop: Single person, straight-on portrait, head and upper shoulders. Face centered horizontally and vertically with both eyes clearly visible. Keep all hair or headwear, ears if uncovered, and chin fully inside the frame. Generous clean safe margin around the complete head for a circular avatar crop. The head occupies roughly 60 percent of image height, never an extreme close-up.
Scene and backdrop: Plain pale cool gray-blue studio background, even and uncluttered.
Lighting and mood: Soft natural window light, balanced gentle shadows, friendly restrained closed-mouth smile, relaxed expression and natural posture.
Photography: Realistic 85mm portrait camera photograph, detailed natural human skin texture, visible small natural imperfections, believable eyes and facial anatomy, understated professional styling. Modest navy and off-white business clothing as specified.
Constraints: One person only. One square individual photo only. No text, letters, logos or watermark. No collage, grid, multiple views or paired portraits. No illustration, cartoon, 3D render, clay, plastic skin, beauty filter, glamour retouch, heavy cosmetics or artificial oversmoothing. Do not imitate a known person.
```
